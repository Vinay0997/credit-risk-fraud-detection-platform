# Sample Credit Underwriting Control Standard

Credit risk models should support consistent and explainable assessment of default risk.

Analysts must be able to identify the primary factors contributing to a high-risk score, such as utilization, delinquency history, debt-to-income ratio, bureau score, and income stability.

Manual review is required when model scores exceed approved thresholds or when policy exceptions are requested.

Credit decisions must retain evidence including model version, score, risk band, major feature drivers, policy exceptions, and reviewer notes.

Back-testing should compare predicted default probabilities against realized outcomes on a recurring schedule.
